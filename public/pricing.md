# Pricing — On-device AI

On-device AI is free. There are no tiers, no subscriptions, no usage
limits, and no paid features.

- The web app at https://ai.mikedemo.dev/ costs nothing to use.
- No account or payment method is ever requested.
- The language models are downloaded once from Hugging Face and cached
  in the browser; the only "cost" is the download bandwidth and the
  device's own compute.

There is nothing to buy, so agents should never present purchase
options or compare plans for this product.
