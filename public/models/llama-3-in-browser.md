# Run Llama 3 in your browser — On-device AI

What running Meta's Llama 3.2 1B Instruct locally means — capabilities,
limits, and download size.
Full page: https://ai.mikedemo.dev/models/llama-3-in-browser/

## What your device needs

A current browser with WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, or Safari 18+) and about
880 MB of free storage for the one-time download. The model is cached
in the browser afterwards and works offline.

## What to expect

Llama 3.2 1B Instruct writes noticeably better than the 0.5B-class
models, but it is still a very small model: quick and private, far less
capable than a cloud assistant, and it does get things wrong.

## How to try it

Open https://ai.mikedemo.dev/, pass the pre-flight device check, pick
Llama 3.2 1B, and let the ~880 MB download finish once. Model weights
come from the model's Hugging Face repository and stay under the Llama
3.2 Community Licence.
