# Open source & credits — On-device AI

Open-source licenses and third-party credits.
Full page: https://ai.mikedemo.dev/licenses/

## Libraries (from the in-app credits)

- **Web Awesome** — Font Awesome / Fonticons, Inc. — MIT. The component
  library behind the interface.
- **Font Awesome Free** — Fonticons, Inc. — CC BY 4.0 (icons),
  SIL OFL 1.1 (fonts), MIT (code).
- **React** — Meta and contributors — MIT.
- **TanStack Start & Router** — Tanner Linsley and contributors — MIT.
- **WebLLM** (@mlc-ai/web-llm) — MLC AI — Apache 2.0. Graphics-accelerated
  inference via WebGPU.
- **wllama** (@wllama/wllama) — MIT, wrapping llama.cpp (MIT).
  Processor-only inference via WebAssembly.

## Models

Model weights are downloaded at runtime from their own hosts and remain
under their own licences: Qwen2.5 (Apache 2.0), Llama 3.2 (Llama 3.2
Community Licence), Phi-4 mini (MIT), SmolLM2 (Apache 2.0). No model
weights are committed to the repository.

## Digital carbon

The site also reports its own digital carbon footprint — the cost of
serving a static page versus running inference on the visitor's device.
